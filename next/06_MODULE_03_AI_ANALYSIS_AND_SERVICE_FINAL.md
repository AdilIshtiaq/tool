# Module 3 — AI Analysis and Service Recommendation

## Purpose

Understand the business and identify the best commercial opportunity.

## Inputs

- lead data
- approved website/business information
- analysis rules
- service catalog

## Analysis

AI should identify:
- website issues
- UX opportunities
- content opportunities
- SEO opportunities
- conversion opportunities
- online presence gaps
- other approved opportunity categories

## Output

- summary
- opportunity score
- confidence
- reasons
- evidence
- missing information
- recommended service
- secondary services
- next action

## Service catalog

Services are configured by the admin.

AI must select only from enabled services.

## Rule builder

User can:
- create rules manually
- ask AI for suggested rules
- edit suggestions
- enable/disable rules

## Review policy

Low confidence results go to `Needs Review`.

High-impact recommendations can also require manual approval.

## Explainability

Every recommendation must have a `Why this service?` explanation.
