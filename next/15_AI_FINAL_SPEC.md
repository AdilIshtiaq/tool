# Final AI Specification

## Global AI behavior

AI must:
- use provided information
- clearly mark missing information
- avoid invented facts
- return structured output
- provide reasons
- provide confidence when applicable

## AI analysis

Input:
- lead
- allowed business information
- analysis rules
- service catalog

Output:
- summary
- opportunities
- score
- confidence
- evidence
- recommendation
- missing information
- next action

## AI service recommendation

The recommended service must exist in the service catalog.

## AI email

AI can:
- create a draft
- personalize a template
- suggest subject lines

AI must not invent:
- client names
- existing relationships
- results
- prices
- technical facts not present in the input

## AI call script

The script must be based on the actual analysis.

## AI reply classification

Return:
- category
- confidence
- summary
- suggested action
- review flag

## Human feedback

Store:
- AI recommendation
- human decision
- optional rejection reason

This allows future measurement of AI accuracy.
