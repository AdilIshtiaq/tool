# Module 6 — Manual Calling Workspace

## Purpose

Give the salesperson everything needed for a manual call.

## Workspace

### Lead panel
- Business name
- Contact name
- Phone
- Address
- Website
- Email

### Intelligence panel
- AI summary
- Recommended service
- Opportunity score
- Reason for recommendation

### Calling panel
- Reason for calling
- Call objective
- Script
- Editable script
- Notes
- Outcome
- Follow-up date

## AI call script

Script should include:
1. Opening
2. Reason for calling
3. Relevant business observation
4. Value statement
5. Discovery questions
6. Objection prompts
7. Next step

## Call outcome

- No answer
- Connected
- Interested
- Follow-up
- Meeting booked
- Not interested
- Wrong number

## Important

The user makes the call manually.

The system records the call activity but does not place the call in the first version.

## Timeline

Every saved call becomes a lead timeline event.
