# CRM and Lead Timeline

## Pipeline

New
→ Qualified
→ Analyzed
→ Contacted
→ Interested
→ Follow-up
→ Meeting
→ Proposal
→ Won / Lost

## Timeline events

Record:
- lead discovered
- qualification
- AI analysis
- recommendation
- email
- reply
- call
- note
- task
- stage change

## Lead profile

One page should provide the full history.

## Tasks

A task contains:
- title
- lead
- owner
- due date
- priority
- status
- notes

## Stage changes

Every important stage change should be logged.
